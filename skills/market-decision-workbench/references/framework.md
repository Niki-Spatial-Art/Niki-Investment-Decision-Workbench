# Distilled Market Framework

## Two Separate Systems

### 1. Tactical execution

- Sequence: market filter -> theme strength -> relative-strength candidate -> entry only after a pullback/consolidation -> predefined invalidation.
- Useful observations: rising volume on breakout, declining volume during orderly consolidation, and renewed volume on confirmation.
- Non-transferable claims: exact intraday times, fixed turnover thresholds, or assertions that a large down day is necessarily "washing".
- Avoid short-term trading in a one-way decline, during an unconfirmed base, or when the user cannot monitor and execute the plan.

### 2. Long-horizon research

- Begin with first principles: customer need, value-chain bottleneck, pricing power, competition, capital intensity, and growth duration.
- Assess valuation with both current realizable business and separately valued optionality. Do not price remote narratives as current earnings.
- Use penetration carefully: low penetration can support optionality; high penetration plus high valuation needs unusually strong earnings durability.
- Evaluate payoff as upside magnitude, downside magnitude, probability uncertainty, and position size. Low price alone is not high expected value.

## Market-State Checklist

| Check | What it tells you | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Index trend | Broad regime and downside risk | A single-day rebound is mistaken for a turn |
| Breadth | Participation versus narrow leadership | One hot sector masks broad weakness |
| Turnover/liquidity | Whether moves have sponsorship | Volume rises only in distribution or event trading |
| Leadership | Which themes can carry risk | Confuse a news spike with a durable leader |
| Macro/event risk | Discount-rate and liquidity shocks | Ignore earnings, IPO, policy, or overseas risk events |
| Account exposure | Whether a new trade adds a new risk | Count correlated tech/semiconductor positions as diversification |

## Regime-to-Action Map

| Regime | Evidence | Default action |
|---|---|---|
| Risk-on | Trend confirmed, breadth supportive, leadership stable | Hold winners; add only to planned pullbacks |
| Transition | Some stabilization but confirmation incomplete | Build watchlist; exploratory positions only if risk budget exists |
| Bottoming/uncertain | Index/breadth disagree or event risk dominates | Keep risk low; no chase; prioritize cash management |
| Risk-off | Trend breaks with weak breadth and persistent selling | Reduce risk; do not bottom-fish mechanically |

## Red Flags

- A claim gives a ticker but no source, valuation, earnings evidence, or invalidation condition.
- A position is justified only by a group-chat consensus or a prior gain.
- A new purchase duplicates an existing factor/theme risk bucket.
- A return target demands an implausibly high recurring monthly return; reset expected return and drawdown assumptions before trading.
